# Animus Companion v7.6.8.1 — Route Planner Viewport Refinement

## Phase 1 adjustment

- Increased the iPhone route planner's usable vertical space.
- Sized the scrollable stop list so three complete route-stop cards can be viewed before additional scrolling is required on standard 430 × 771 iPhone viewports.
- Preserved the sticky header and Reverse, Optimize, and Clear Route action bar.
- Added a smaller-height fallback for compact landscape or short browser viewports.
- No route logic, map calibration, database records, storage keys, or user-data schemas were changed.
